# Claim-first research workflow

1. Write the precise assertion and relevant period. Identify the intended entity and approved name variants.
2. Select a source category that can address that assertion. A company identifier, appointment, publication and market-leadership assertion require different evidence.
3. Check that the destination covers the right jurisdiction/entity class. ADGM is not all UAE; the Canadian federal database is not every Canadian corporation.
4. Inspect the actual record. Preserve its URL, access date, relevant passage/location and identity matching rationale.
5. Distinguish declarations, filings, aggregated metadata and independently reported evidence. Their provenance and limits differ.
6. Record support, partial support, contradiction or unreviewed status. A missing record in a limited database is not proof that the entity does not exist.
7. Use the [Authority Evidence Mapper](https://github.com/mhdteto/authority-evidence-mapper) to organize the claim-level review, preserving contrary sources and unknown relationships.

Do not publish unnecessary personal data or confidential documents in a public issue. The catalogue creates no entitlement to a listing, record modification, citation or backlink. No external service is queried by the compiler.
